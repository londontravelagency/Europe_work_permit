FAQ

Before you get in touch

What happens at the first consultation?
You explain your situation, we ask the questions that actually decide the case, and you leave with a realistic view of your options. If we cannot help, we say so at that meeting.
How much will my case cost?
You receive a written scope and a fixed fee before any chargeable work begins. If the case later changes shape, we agree any variation with you in writing first.
My application was refused. Is it over?
Not necessarily. Refusals often turn on missing evidence or a misread rule rather than the merits. Bring the refusal notice and we will tell you whether it is worth challenging.
Do I have to come to your London office?
No. Many clients work with us entirely remotely by WhatsApp, email and video call, sending documents electronically. In-person appointments are available if you prefer.
Will my information stay private?
Yes. Your file is handled on a strict need-to-know basis and is never discussed with family, employers or third parties without your written instruction.
Can you guarantee my visa will be granted?
No adviser honestly can. Decisions rest with the Home Office. What we control is the quality, accuracy and completeness of what is put in front of the decision maker.
Do you help with document attestation and translation?
Yes. We coordinate certified copies, translations and legalisation, and refer you to a notary where a notarial act is required.

This page is general information about our services. It is not legal advice for your circumstances and no adviser-client relationship is created until we have agreed a scope of work with you in writing.
