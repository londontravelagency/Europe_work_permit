Checklist

Documents you will need

Your exact list depends on your nationality, course level and sponsor. This is the standard core file we build for UK Student Visa applicants.

Valid passport

Machine-readable, with enough validity for the full course.

CAS from your university

Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies — issued after you accept an offer.

Academic transcripts & certificates

All previous qualifications, plus certified translations where needed.

English language evidence

IELTS / SELT or an approved university-assessed exemption.

Financial evidence

Tuition and living costs held for the required qualifying period.

TB test certificate

Required from many countries, from a UKVI-approved clinic.

Biometrics & photograph

Appointment booked at your local visa application centre.

Sponsorship / no-objection letters

Where a third party, employer or government is funding you.

FAQ

Questions students ask us most

How much does the whole process cost?
Costs fall into three parts: our service fee, the university tuition deposit, and the official UKVI fees. We give you a single written breakdown before you commit, with no charges added later.
Do I definitely need IELTS?
Not always. Some institutions accept their own internal English assessment or accept prior study in English. We check what your chosen institution will accept before you spend money on a test.
How long should I hold my funds in the bank?
Financial evidence must normally be held for a continuous qualifying period ending shortly before you apply. The exact period and amount are set by UKVI, so we confirm the current requirement for your case.
Can I work while studying?
Most students on degree-level courses at recognised institutions may work limited hours during term and full time in vacations. Your visa will state your exact permission, and we explain it clearly before you travel.
Can my spouse or children come with me?
Dependant rights are now limited to specific course types. We assess this honestly at the first consultation rather than after you have paid a deposit.
What happens if my visa is refused?
We review the refusal notice with you, identify whether the issue can be cured, and advise on re-application or an administrative review. Our legal team handles this in-house.
Do you guarantee a visa?
No one can. Admission decisions rest with the institution and visa decisions rest with the Home Office. What we guarantee is a complete, accurate and honestly prepared application.
